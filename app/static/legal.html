Data and legal

Last updated 10 October 2026

memeclip is a personal, non-commercial project: a search engine for short meme clips in Hindi and English, built as portfolio and research work. It has no ads, no payments and no user accounts. This page says where the clips come from, who owns them, and how to ask for something to be removed.

The clips belong to their owners

  • Most meme clips are cut from films, TV shows and online videos. memeclip does not own them and holds no licence for them. Copyright stays with the studios, broadcasters and creators who made them.
  • The search tool itself, meaning the code, the ranking and the tagging, is the project's own work. The clips are not part of it.
  • The public source code contains no clips. Videos, thumbnails and face photos stay on the machine that runs memeclip and are kept out of the repository.
  • The running site is private: searching, uploading and editing need the owner's login. A single clip becomes reachable by anyone only when the owner shares its link (/c/…). Each shared clip remains its owner's work, and the shared page links back to the source when the source is known.

Where the data comes from

  • Clips added by hand, together with the title, caption and comments that came with them.
  • Reddit, through Reddit's official Data API and only once Reddit has approved access. It is used non-commercially and within Reddit's rate limits. Clips are not used to train models unless Reddit agrees to it. If a Reddit post is deleted, its clip is removed on request, and an automatic check for this is planned.
  • Clips are not collected by getting around a platform's blocks or rate limits.

People and faces

  • memeclip can recognise well-known public figures, such as actors in famous scenes, so a search like "nana patekar" finds their clips. It matches faces against a small gallery of photos that the owner adds, and also finds names in titles and captions.
  • Face matching runs only on the owner's machine. Face photos and face data are never published or shared, and are not for sale.
  • memeclip is not for identifying private individuals. Faces it does not recognise get a name only if the owner adds one by hand.
  • Recognising someone helps people find a clip. It does not mean that person endorses memeclip, and their name and image are not used to promote anything.
  • The face models (InsightFace) are published for non-commercial research use, which is another reason memeclip stays non-commercial.

Asking for removal

If you own a clip, appear in one, or posted the original and want it taken down, open an issue at github.com/RorschachTest/giffy-giffy/issues with the share link or a description of the clip. Clips are removed without argument, usually within a few days. A person's photos and face data are removed from the gallery in the same way.

If this ever became a product

Running memeclip as a business would need more than this page: licences for any clip catalogue it offered, or a model where users upload their own clips backed by a working takedown process; the duties India's IT Rules 2021 place on platforms, including a grievance officer; compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act; and permission to use well-known people's names and likeness commercially. None of that is in place, which is why memeclip stays a non-commercial project.

This page describes how the project is run. It is not legal advice, and it does not grant anyone rights to the clips.
