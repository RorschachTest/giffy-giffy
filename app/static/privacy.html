Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

memeclip is a small, non-commercial project for searching Hindi and English meme clips. This page says what it collects about you, why, and how to have it removed. It does not sell your data, show ads, or share your data with advertisers.

What we collect when you sign in

You sign in with Google. memeclip asks Google only for your basic profile, and nothing else (no contacts, no Gmail, no Drive, no calendar):

  • Email address, and that Google has verified it
  • Name (full, first and last), as on your Google account
  • Profile picture (a link to it, shown next to your name)
  • Language, when Google shares it
  • Google account ID, a number that keeps your account the same if your email changes

We also keep when your account was created and when you last signed in, your role (user or admin) and whether your account is active, on the waitlist or blocked.

What we keep while you use it

  • A sign-in cookie (mc_session) that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. The server keeps only a scrambled copy of it. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Searches: what was searched and how many clips were found, used to see what people look for and what is missing. They are stored without your name.
  • Shares: which clip was shared, used to show popular clips first.

Why

To let you in, to keep the number of people within what the project can run (there is a limit and a waitlist), to keep the site safe from misuse, and to improve search. Nothing else.

Who else handles it

  • Google confirms who you are when you sign in.
  • Cloudflare carries the site's traffic securely between your browser and the server.

The clips themselves are described with the help of Google's Gemini models through Vercel's AI Gateway; that uses the clips' text and pictures, never your account or your searches.

How long we keep it

Your account stays until you ask for it to be deleted. Sign-in cookies expire after 30 days or when you sign out. Search records are not linked to your account.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time with your picture in the top bar; this ends the session.
  • Delete your account: open a request at github.com/RorschachTest/giffy-giffy/issues with the email you sign in with. It is deleted within 7 days.
  • Remove memeclip's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. For what the clips are and where they come from, see Data and legal.
